YouTube Live chat text to speech: how to read chat out loud

August 2026 · Guide

YouTube Live chat moves fast, especially during premieres and high-traffic streams. Reading it out loud with TikLive TTS lets you react to messages without looking away from your camera or whatever you're showing on screen.

How to enable TTS on your YouTube Live chat

  1. Download TikLive TTS (free, Windows) from the home page.
  2. Connect your YouTube channel or live stream from the app.
  3. Pick a voice and language — 13 languages available.
  4. Turn on the anti-spam filter so repeated messages or bot spam don't interrupt the reading.

Why use TTS on YouTube Live

  • Never miss messages even when chat moves faster than you can read on screen.
  • Multistream-friendly: if you also stream on TikTok Live and Twitch, TikLive TTS unifies all three chats into a single queue — see the multi-platform chat guide.
  • OBS overlays included, if you want to show alerts and counters on screen during the stream.
