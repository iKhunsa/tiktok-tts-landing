TikTok Live chat text to speech: how to set it up

August 2026 · Guide

Staring at chat the whole time you're live on TikTok pulls you away from the camera. Reading TikTok Live chat out loud with a text-to-speech (TTS) tool fixes that: you hear messages while still looking at your audience, without losing the thread of the stream.

What you need for TikTok Live chat text to speech

With TikLive TTS there's no account, sign-up or server setup required. It's a free Windows desktop app that connects directly to your TikTok Live stream and starts reading messages as soon as they come in.

Steps

  1. Download TikLive TTS (free) from the home page and install it.
  2. Open the app and connect your TikTok Live username — no need to log in with your TikTok account, just point it at the live to follow.
  3. Pick a voice and language (13 languages available) to match your audience.
  4. Turn on the anti-spam filter so the app ignores repeated messages, overly long text, or blocked words.
  5. Go live — messages are read out loud in real time, in the order they arrive.

Advantages over reading chat manually

  • You keep eye contact with the camera while chat stays active.
  • You react faster to gifts, questions and mentions because you hear them right away.
  • Works alongside Twitch and YouTube if you stream on multiple platforms at once — TikLive TTS can unify them into a single queue (see the multi-platform chat guide).
